## Description English
```text
Search boxes right on the shop, construction and map screens. Click the box and type: the list updates as you type.

- Shop: filter a category or brand, or search the whole shop, also by brand, DLC, mod name and mod author.
- Spec filters: price ($<50k), horsepower (hp>200), working width (width>9), capacity (l>30000), weight, speed, power needed and "affordable", mixed with words: "fendt hp>300 $<400k".
- Construction: filter the buildings in every category and tab by name or product: "peas" finds every factory that uses or makes peas.
- Map: find your vehicles (also by license plate), bales, pallets, buildings, fields and properties. Every match gets an arrow, and the nearest are listed under the box.
- Map searches like "wheat ready", "sell wheat" (best price marked), "for sale $<100k ha>5", "contract", "fuel<20" and "stored wheat".
- Settings > Controls gets a search box too.

How to use: Ctrl+F (or a right stick click on a controller) jumps into the search box. Enter keeps the search, Esc clears it. Everything is explained in the game's Help menu under Searchables.
```

## Screenshots

Take them in game, in a save with **only base-game content and DTM Searchables** (all other mods off: no AutoDrive markers, modded vehicles or other map icons in the picture). Save as 1920x1080 JPG. Don't add text, captions, borders or logos afterwards: ModHub refuses any image changed outside the game.

1. **No HUD, no text:** the shop with the search box in use, e.g. "fendt hp>300" typed and the filtered list showing. (The first screenshot must not have text added; the game's own menu is the mod here.)
2. Map: "sell wheat" with the result list and the arrows.
3. Map: "for sale" listing land to buy.
4. Map: your vehicles found (e.g. "tractor"), arrows on them.
5. Shop: "seeder width>9".
6. Construction: "peas" filtering the production buildings.

At least three are required; more that show different features help. The old screenshots in `screenshots/` have caption bars and many other mods in view: don't upload them.
